Our Story

Born From Passion,
Built on Fire

Omar started Al-Mazag three years ago in Moharram Bek with a small grill, a bold recipe, and one promise: every burger should feel like more than just food.

Today Al-Mazag is one of Alexandria’s favorite burger spots. Omar’s young restaurant is built on fresh ingredients, warm service, and a growing local reputation.
